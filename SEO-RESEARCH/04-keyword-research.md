## 2. Indian Keyword Landscape (Market B)

India represents one of the largest single pools of typing test search volume globally due to mandatory computer typing qualifications in central and state government employment.

### Central Government Recruitment Exams (SSC)

|---|---|---|---|

| `ssc chsl typing test` | Transactional / Exam Prep | `/ssc-typing-test/` | 10 minutes, 35 WPM in English (10,500 KDPH) or 30 WPM in Hindi (9,000 KDPH). |
