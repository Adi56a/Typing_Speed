## 2. Competitive Weaknesses We Capitalize On

1. **Typing.com's Commercial Distraction**:
   - Typing.com is loaded with video ads, banner ads, and third-party trackers.
   - *Our Edge*: CheckTypingSpeeds provides a clutter-free, lightning-fast, ad-free learning interface with 0 tracking scripts.
2. **Monkeytype's Lack of Guidance & Certification**:
   - Monkeytype is outstanding for enthusiasts, but completely lacks beginner touch-typing lessons, educational explanations, and verifiable certificate generation.
   - *Our Edge*: We provide Monkeytype's sleek fluidity combined with full touch-typing curriculum and formal certificate cards.
3. **TypingTest.com's Cluttered Layout & High CLS**:
   - TypingTest.com suffers from aggressive ad loading that disrupts the typing experience.
   - *Our Edge*: Perfect 0.00 CLS, instant hydration, and 100% pre-rendered indexable speed.
4. **TypingMaster India's Outdated Desktop Gate**:
   - Most Indian exam candidates are forced to download cumbersome trial desktop software or deal with low-quality ad blogs.
   - *Our Edge*: High-precision, browser-based SSC CGL DEST and CHSL exam simulation with authentic 15-minute 2,000 keystroke passage evaluation.
